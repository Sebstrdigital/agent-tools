# Discovery

Be a thinking partner, not a new questionnaire. Establish purpose from discussion, project knowledge and inspected evidence. Ask one consequential question at a time; do not ask again for settled decisions. A small understood task can proceed directly to a recommendation.

## Reach a useful decision

State the decision and intended customer outcome briefly. Distinguish facts, assumptions and material unknowns. Inspect relevant rules/source and recall useful Munin decisions before asking the user. Choose a bounded experiment only if it could change the decision: question, comparator, sufficient-evidence criteria, relevant checks, permissions and a simple budget/stopping rule. A few sentences normally suffice.

At blockers, consider a cheaper safe alternative or whether stopping with uncertainty is more useful than fixing infrastructure. A failed or inconclusive experiment can be valid evidence. Park unrelated improvements; do not build a benchmark project or planning suite by default.

## Recommend, then let the user choose

Explain one recommendation; the user selects.

- **Direct:** current-agent work with a concise approved task and relevant checks. No mandatory files.
- **Developer:** one separate builder using the explicitly approved compact packet and dedicated `developer` tool. See `../../docs/lean-workflows.md` for the invocation when needed. Do not bypass Team-only controls.
- **Team:** fresh independent validation/review when useful. Selection authorizes preparation only. Use the delivery skill and one compact task record; approve its actual Agreement section before launching. Brief/Plan/Agreement are not three mandatory files.

Do not automatically select Team. If important uncertainty remains, recommend discovery rather than a premature builder. A short durable note is enough for a long investigation; do not create a planning suite.

## Context and autonomy

Approve the useful outcome and meaningful boundaries, including ordinary diagnosis and bounded repair—not each command. Use meaningful thin vertical slices with early actual-boundary feedback; fakes are limited evidence. Refine from results, not a majority-token upfront planning rule.

Supply purpose, constraints, acceptance and relevant rules/source pointers. Read source/evidence as needed. No mandatory reading manifests, read receipts or historical rereading; excerpts must not omit governing constraints. Keep logs on disk and use codemode for bounded retrieval.

Detail follows consequence and reversibility. Do not require unrelated applications to close; isolate live-side-effect tests and obtain approval if required checks change. Commit/push/install/deploy permissions remain explicit unless already approved.

## Scout and handoff

For a bounded supplied-source question, Scout may investigate without Developer or Team selection. Scripts first for deterministic extraction/counting. Supply discoveryAuthorityRef, a focused question, accessible sources, relevant rules, exclusions, budget and cited resultContract. The lead verifies actual permission; metadata is not consent. Scout is read-only, does not accept work or replace Team gates. Consult `../../docs/workflows/scout-role-routing.md` only for runtime/grant/routing details.

Narrate meaningful findings, not every tool call. Record concise decisions, outcomes and next steps in Munin without secrets; use exact IDs for known handoffs and check current state. Static checks and model observations are not universal behavior guarantees.
